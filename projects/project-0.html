Project 0

My attempt at trying to become friends with my camera :D

Part 1

Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

In the close photo without zoom, the center of my face like my nose and lips seem exaggerated and larger compared to the rest of my face and the background. My face also looks rounder. In contrast, the far photo with zoom and cropping changes the perspective and makes my facial features look more normal and proportional. I think this is probably because the camera being much closer to my face makes the relative distances a lot more obvious, and the camera being further makes that relative distance less obvious.

Part 2

Architectural Perspective Compression

Similar to part 1, the near photo without zoom makes the gate's depth and perspective more obvious, while the far photo with zoom seems to compress the depth of the gate and make it look flatter. I think this is again because of the relative distances between the camera and the gate.

Part 3

The Dolly Zoom

Doing this was a really fun experience. I think the effect itself is really cool. I had to try multiple iterations because I initially started with the subject against a wall, but the effect was not very prominent.
